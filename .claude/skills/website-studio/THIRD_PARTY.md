## Project responsibilities

- Record the origin and usage basis of external media, fonts, icons, logos, copy, and data.
- Treat publicly searchable material as discoverable, not automatically licensed for republication.
- Treat user-provided material as publishable only when the provider confirms ownership or authority for the intended use.
