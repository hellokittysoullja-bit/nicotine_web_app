# Landing Page Recipe

## User Job

Understand the offer quickly, decide whether it is relevant and credible, and take one primary action.

## Fast or Capability Path

Use the fast path for a focused static page with known content and a simple link or externally handled form. Use the capability path for personalization, account creation, payments, multi-step forms, localization, content management, experimentation, or operational integrations.

## Recommended Structure

1. Compact navigation with one primary action.
2. Hero stating audience, value, differentiation, and action.
3. Immediate proof: product evidence, outcomes, or credible trust signals.
4. Problem and benefit sequence.
5. How it works or key capabilities.
6. Focused proof such as a case study, demo, or attributed testimonial.
7. Objection handling, pricing context, or FAQ.
8. Final action and trust-bearing footer.

Remove any section that repeats the same claim without adding evidence.

## Content and Data Needs

- specific audience and value proposition;
- one primary conversion action;
- product or service evidence;
- honest differentiators and limitations;
- approved brand assets and imagery;
- real proof with permission to publish;
- pricing, availability, terms, privacy, and contact details when relevant;
- form destination, data handling, and follow-up ownership.

## Visual Traps

- vague headline over unrelated stock imagery;
- several equally prominent calls to action;
- feature-card repetition without narrative hierarchy;
- fabricated logos, testimonials, ratings, or metrics;
- animation that delays comprehension;
- oversized hero treatment that pushes all proof below the fold;
- fashionable effects that do not match the offer.

## Interactions and States

Test navigation, anchor links, primary and secondary actions, forms, pricing or comparison toggles, disclosures, and external links. Forms need validation, submitting, success, error, and retry behavior. Optional widgets must fail without blocking the core action.

## Responsive and Accessibility Checks

- The value proposition and primary action fit comfortably on a narrow phone.
- Proof content follows the claim it supports after columns collapse.
- Forms use persistent labels and appropriate input semantics.
- Menus open, close, and restore focus predictably.
- Motion respects reduced-motion settings.
- Text remains readable over all imagery and at increased zoom.

## Done Criteria

- [ ] A new visitor can identify audience, value, and next action in one brief scan.
- [ ] Every important claim has visible support.
- [ ] The primary action works through success or an honest handoff.
- [ ] No placeholder proof, dead link, or unhandled form state remains.
- [ ] Desktop, tablet, and phone compositions have been visually reviewed.
- [ ] Metadata and the deployed access model match the final offer.
