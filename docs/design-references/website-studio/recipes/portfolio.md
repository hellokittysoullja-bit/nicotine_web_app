# Portfolio Recipe

## User Job

Assess the creator or team's point of view, quality, role, and relevance, then decide whether to explore deeper or make contact.

## Fast or Capability Path

Use the fast path for a curated static portfolio with a small number of projects and a direct contact route. Use the capability path for a CMS, many case-study routes, gated work, advanced filtering, multilingual content, private client material, or integrated inquiries.

## Recommended Structure

1. Identity-led hero with discipline, point of view, and availability or current focus.
2. Selected work with the strongest project first.
3. Case studies explaining context, role, process, result, and credit.
4. Capabilities or working approach.
5. Concise biography and credibility.
6. Optional attributed testimonials or client context.
7. Clear contact invitation and practical contact method.

Prioritize a few well-explained projects over a large unexplained gallery.

## Content and Data Needs

- preferred name, role, location or working context when useful;
- concise positioning statement;
- project title, client or context, date, role, collaborators, challenge, contribution, and outcome;
- approved images, video, prototypes, and credits;
- confidentiality boundaries and permission to publish;
- contact route, availability, and expected follow-up;
- downloadable résumé or profile only when maintained and accessible.

## Visual Traps

- work hidden below a self-indulgent oversized introduction;
- gallery tiles without role, context, or outcome;
- excessive device mockups that obscure the work;
- scroll effects that make projects difficult to revisit;
- navigation or project titles visible only on hover;
- inconsistent image crops that weaken comparison;
- presenting collaborative work as sole authorship.

## Interactions and States

Project cards, case-study navigation, filters, galleries, lightboxes, media playback, downloads, and contact forms must work with keyboard and touch. Handle missing media, slow images, empty filter results, unavailable private work, form submission, errors, and return navigation.

## Responsive and Accessibility Checks

- The first strong project appears early on phone and desktop.
- Project order remains intentional after columns collapse.
- Captions, roles, and credits remain visible without hover.
- Galleries preserve focal content and offer usable controls and alternatives.
- Video is captioned or accompanied by equivalent context where needed.
- Contact actions and downloadable files have descriptive labels.

## Done Criteria

- [ ] The portfolio communicates a distinctive point of view and a credible role.
- [ ] Every featured project explains contribution and outcome.
- [ ] Credits and confidentiality boundaries are accurate.
- [ ] Work samples load, preserve intended crops, and remain navigable without hover.
- [ ] Contact or inquiry flow completes successfully.
- [ ] Wide and narrow visual reviews confirm hierarchy, image quality, and reading order.
