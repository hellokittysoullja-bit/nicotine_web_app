# Editorial Story Recipe

## User Job

Understand a subject through a guided, emotionally coherent narrative while retaining clear paths to evidence, deeper exploration, and source context.

## Fast or Capability Path

Use the fast path for a single researched story, profile, anniversary page, or archive with static content and authorized embeds. Use the capability path for many stories, search, a content-management workflow, interactive datasets, personalization, accounts, or complex exploratory tools.

## Recommended Structure

Choose only chapters supported by meaningful material:

1. Emotional promise and clear entry into the story.
2. Thesis explaining why the subject matters.
3. One defining idea presented through explanation or focused interaction.
4. Chronology, phases, or milestones.
5. People, contributors, or perspectives.
6. Artifacts, documents, places, or process evidence.
7. Selected authorized media.
8. Sources, credits, uncertainty, and rights context.

Vary composition and pacing across chapters instead of repeating one card grid.

## Content and Data Needs

- editorial thesis and intended audience;
- sourced names, dates, quotations, credits, and milestones;
- clear distinction between confirmed fact, interpretation, unresolved information, and opinion;
- media inventory with creator, source, rights status, attribution, and intended use;
- spoiler, sensitivity, privacy, or cultural-context boundaries;
- authorship, review ownership, corrections path, and source links;
- authorized embed identifiers rather than local copies of protected media.

## Visual Traps

- using generic stock or unrelated art as the main emotional image;
- turning every chapter into the same rounded card grid;
- decorative archival or technical motifs with no relationship to the subject;
- cropping people, documents, posters, or group compositions carelessly;
- overwhelming evidence with texture, glow, grain, or motion;
- presenting uncertainty as fact;
- mirroring protected text, audio, or video because the project is noncommercial.

## Interactions and States

Use timelines, tabs, filters, maps, media selectors, annotations, or reveal controls only when they improve understanding. Provide obvious active states, keyboard behavior, and useful content when scripts or embeds fail. Sensitive reveals must be explicit and reversible. Handle unavailable media, empty filters, unresolved entries, and external-source failures.

## Responsive and Accessibility Checks

- The title, thesis, and key image remain legible and compositionally intact on narrow screens.
- Long-form text uses a readable measure and heading structure.
- Timelines and dense diagrams recompose rather than shrink.
- Captions, credits, and source links remain adjacent to their material.
- Interactive concepts expose selected, expanded, or pressed state appropriately.
- Embeds have titles, fallbacks, and stable aspect ratios.
- Reduced-motion mode preserves the narrative without effects.

## Done Criteria

- [ ] The opening establishes subject, perspective, and reason to continue.
- [ ] Every chapter advances the narrative or provides evidence.
- [ ] Facts, interpretation, uncertainty, and opinion are distinguishable.
- [ ] Every shipped or embedded media item has traceable rights handling.
- [ ] Key imagery is relevant, credited, and correctly composed at all tested sizes.
- [ ] Interactive sections work with pointer and keyboard and degrade safely.
- [ ] Source, correction, authorship, and rights context are easy to find.
